The concept

The mark reads left to right as a story: a loose, human waveform — one person's raw idea — resolves into a structured cluster of connected nodes. Purple is the individual signal. The red-to-orange gradient is what happens when that signal joins the network: it gets shape, connection, and attribution. This cover carries the same arc — a waveform rising from the individual (purple) into the network (ember) — so the same logic that drives the logo drives every asset in the system.
