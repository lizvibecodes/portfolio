The Concept

The mark reads left to right as a story: a loose, human waveform; one person's raw idea; resolves into a structured cluster of connected nodes. Purple is the individual signal. The red-to-orange gradient is what happens when that signal joins the network: it gets shape, connection, and attribution. This cover carries the same arc; a waveform rising from the individual (purple) into the network (ember); so the same logic that drives the logo drives every asset in the system.

The Challenge

Founders needed a professional, trusted visual presence to build early anticipation and engage prospective partners without exposing sensitive proprietary concepts ahead of launch.

My approach

I designed a clean, minimalistic identity system centered around a dynamic geometric logo symbolizing flow, connection, and clarity. Supported by bold typography and a secure, collaborative aesthetic, the visual system establishes orvelle as an authoritative, professional digital space.

The Result

Provided immediate visual credibility for pre-launch outreach, enabling the founders to confidently pitch strategic partnerships and attract early-stage adopters
