The Concept

Root & Radiance had a clear vision for holistic self-care, but their identity needed to translate that warmth and luxury into a tangible system. I developed an earthy color palette, elegant typography, and subtle touches of luxury, choosing every detail from the logo to a refined tone of voice to reflect authenticity and upscale appeal.

The Challenge

Their social media materials were scattered and lacked cohesion. Different fonts, layouts, and text treatments, with no clear standards, created a disjointed digital presence that made it difficult for audiences to recognize or connect with the brand.

My approach

I built a unified visual framework anchored by a polished, harmonious design system. Every touchpoint was thoughtfully aligned to embody the salon's commitment to nourishment, transformation, and intention.

The Result

The updated visuals and messaging amplify warmth and clarity, helping Root & Radiance stand out as both inviting and luxurious, and ensuring the brand radiates as brightly as its promise.
